## Revised prompt v1
Create a polished single-player 2D mobile Roblox game called Grow Your Own Planet. Show one large barren planet with rocks and empty pond. One large Collect Water button gives one Water per tap. Show counter and progress to five Water. At five, unlock Restore Planet; spend five Water to transform terrain into green grass, blue pond, trees and a frog. Animate droplets and transformation. Clear instructions, large mobile buttons, first reward within 15 seconds, celebration, New Planet restart. Implement actual working interaction and visual change, not static scenery. No extra planets, currencies, shops or complex systems.
