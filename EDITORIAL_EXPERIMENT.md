# Editorial experiment

Keep two existing channels and existing render frequency. Rotate town stories, world rules, kingdom stories, power archive and fair-play mysteries within the original channel. Use original characters and locations. Anime remains evidence-led commentary.

For each rendered candidate, review visual continuity, factual support where applicable, originality, audio, captions, and complete payoff. Reject failures. Do not publish from this draft.

After release approval, compare formats using engaged views, average percentage viewed, subscribers gained and returning viewers over comparable seven-day windows. Require at least five published examples per format before making directional decisions; small samples are not proof. Keep experimentation separate from production. No fake engagement, new paid services, or automatic new channel creation. Analytics ingestion and persistent world assets are future work, not implemented here.

Before merging: populate reviewed anime topic packs, run CI, review dry-run videos, verify free-service terms and existing publishing controls.
